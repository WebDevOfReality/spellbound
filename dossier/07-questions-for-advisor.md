# 07: Questions for our interpreter advisor

These are for the first conversation. Record the answers here, with the date, and include anything
they wish we'd asked.

## The kids and families
1. What ages do you work with? Where does fingerspelling come in for them?
2. What do *hearing parents* struggle with most when learning to sign with their child?
3. Would families practice at home on a laptop, a phone, or a school device?
4. Would a kid use this, a parent, or both together? Does that change the design (games vs. drills)?

## Fingerspelling specifics
5. Which letters or transitions do learners get wrong most often?
6. How strict should feedback be for beginners? When does "close enough" help, and when does it hurt?
7. Handedness, regional variation, or classroom conventions we should allow for?
8. Is practicing *reading* fingerspelling (receptive skill) as important as producing it? Should the
   app show fingerspelled words for learners to read?

## Community & trust
9. Who in the Deaf community should review this early? Are there any Deaf educators or orgs you'd
   suggest?
10. What would make you *not* recommend a tool like this to a family?
11. Anything about how tech has been pitched to Deaf kids and families before that we should avoid?

## Practical
12. School device and camera constraints: are webcams blocked, and is there a Chromebook policy?
13. Would you be willing to try iteration 2 (the trainer loop) and give feedback?
