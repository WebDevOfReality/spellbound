# 01: Deaf community & ethics

## Who we're building for

- **Primary users:** hearing family members of Deaf/HH children who are learning ASL, and the kids
  themselves as they learn fingerspelling.
- **Key advisor:** an educational ASL interpreter (Anthony's friend). We need more voices, and
  especially **Deaf** adults and Deaf educators, before any public launch.

Context:
- About 95% of deaf and hard-of-hearing children are born to hearing parents
  ([Mitchell & Karchmer 2004, *Chasing the Mythical Ten Percent*](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ747626)).
- Limited early access to a fully accessible language puts children at risk of language deprivation,
  with effects on cognitive, social-emotional and literacy development
  ([ASHA practice portal](https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/professional-issues/language-communication-deaf-hard-of-hearing-children/);
  [BU, 2017](https://www.bu.edu/articles/2017/asl-language-acquisition)).

## The cautionary tale: sign-language gloves

Hearing-led "sign-to-speech" projects, gloves most famously, have been widely criticized by Deaf
people and linguists. The recurring points:

- **Designed by and for hearing people.** The designers weren't Deaf and didn't involve Deaf users,
  so the product served the hearing side of the conversation
  ([ASHA Leader](https://leader.pubs.asha.org/do/10.1044/leader.IN3.25112020.7/full/);
  [Silent Word](https://silentword.org/why-sign-language-gloves-dont-help-deaf-people/)).
- **They ignore most of the language.** ASL grammar lives in facial expression, body movement and
  space, not only handshapes. Gloves capture only a sliver of it
  ([Liam O'Dell, 2025](https://liamodell.com/2025/01/27/british-sign-language-bsl-artificial-intelligence-ai-chatgpt-signgpt-surrey-oxford-dcal-ucl-deaf-gloves/)).
- **They put the burden on Deaf people** to wear gear so hearing people are spared learning.

## Our commitments

1. **Nothing about us without us.** Deaf advisors review scope, UX, and what counts as "correct".
   Credit them, and pay them when we can.
2. **Honest scope.** We say plainly that Spellbound is a *practice tool*. We never market it as an
   interpreter.
3. **The learner carries the burden, not the Deaf person.** Spellbound helps hearing family members
   learn *their child's* language.
4. **Privacy by default, especially for kids.** Camera frames are processed on-device. No video or
   landmark upload without explicit opt-in, and never for minors without guardian consent.
5. **Respect variation.** Handedness (left-handed signers mirror), regional and personal variation,
   and different hand sizes and skin tones are all valid, so the model must not punish them. We'll
   test for this. See the bias study in [05](05-models-and-runtimes.md).
6. **Data with consent only.** See [03-datasets](03-datasets.md).
